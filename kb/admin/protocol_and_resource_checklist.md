# Institutional protocol and resource checklist for high-risk procedures (synthetic summary)

## ADMIN-KB-05

Any patient with a documented allergic reaction to contrast media requires a formal premedication protocol to be confirmed and recorded before any further contrast-requiring procedure or imaging study is scheduled, regardless of how the previous reaction was managed or how long ago it occurred, since the underlying sensitivity does not resolve with time.

## ADMIN-KB-06

Complex structural heart cases involving competing procedural options, such as a choice between surgical and transcatheter valve treatment, should be reviewed by a multidisciplinary heart team before a final recommendation is finalised, with the review and its conclusion documented in the patient's record.

## ADMIN-KB-07

Before scheduling a high-risk procedure with an elevated predicted risk of post-operative renal injury, confirmation of relevant institutional resources, including intensive care bed availability and renal replacement therapy capacity, should be documented as part of the pre-procedure checklist, not assumed to be available on the basis of general hospital capacity.

## ADMIN-KB-08

A patient retains the right to decline a recommended procedure, including in favour of continued medical management, even where the clinical team's assessment is that intervention offers the better prognosis. This right does not diminish because the patient has previously expressed conditional agreement.
