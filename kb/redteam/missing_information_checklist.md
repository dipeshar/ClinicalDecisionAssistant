# Missing-information checklist for council deliberation (synthetic summary)

## RED-KB-01

A general nephrology follow-up relationship is not the same as a documented, procedure-specific renal risk assessment. If a case notes that a patient is followed by nephrology but the consulting clinician has separately requested formal nephrology input specifically on this procedure, that request being unfulfilled at the time of deliberation is a real, checkable gap.

## RED-KB-02

Institutional resource questions, such as intensive care bed availability, dialysis capacity, and theatre scheduling, are commonly raised in referrals but not always answered within the same document. Their absence from a case should be treated as an open gap requiring confirmation, not as an implicit assumption that resources are adequate.

## RED-KB-03

When a referring clinician explicitly requests a specific piece of further assessment, such as a prehabilitation plan or a fitness evaluation, and that assessment does not appear elsewhere in the case document, this is a genuine missing-information finding rather than an assumption that it was completed informally.

## RED-KB-04

A contrast allergy noted in a patient's history has direct implications for any procedure or workup requiring contrast imaging. If a case discusses a contrast-requiring alternative without explicitly addressing how the allergy will be managed for that specific alternative, this omission is worth flagging even if a premedication protocol exists in principle.
